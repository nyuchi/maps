The tile format

  • Vector tiles in the Mapbox Vector Tile format, addressed as XYZ with the web-mercator grid. The TileJSON is version 3.0.0 and says the scheme is xyz.
  • Tiles are served compressed and can be cached for a long time. The headers below are from a real tile, requested when this page opened.
  • Styles, glyphs and sprites are served beside the tiles and never name a credit themselves: the TileJSON is the only place the credit comes from.

Credits are mandatory

Licence condition

The base map is derived from open data under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), and the tiles are a Produced Work under it. The licence requires the credit wherever the map is shown. This is a condition of using the tiles, not a courtesy.

  1. Show the credit on every map you draw from these tiles, always visible or one tap away behind a small (i).
  2. Take the text and its link from the TileJSON's attribution field. Do not retype it: the field is the one source, and it can change.
  3. MapLibre GL JS, the Leaflet adapter and OpenLayers read the credit from the TileJSON and show it for you. Keep their attribution control switched on.
  4. If you draw tiles yourself, or export an image, put the credit in the picture.
  5. There is no setting to hide the credit on an embed, and there will not be one.
